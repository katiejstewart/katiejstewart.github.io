01 · About

Most of what I have built started as a problem in my own day.

I am a credit analyst at JPMorgan. Alongside the deal work, I kept building tools for the parts of the process that ate the most hours, then sat with the people who had to use them until the new way became the normal way.

I can read a credit memo and I can build the tool that drafts one. I have seen the work from both sides, and that is why adoption, not the build, is the hard part. A workflow nobody opens is not a win, however good the build was.

Before JPMorgan I started WeClean in St. Louis and grew it to 100+ customers with a team of five. I did the hiring, the pricing, the scheduling, and a fair number of the cleans.

In my free time I golf, wakeboard, play squash, and travel about as far south as the map goes.
