01

Technology & Disruptive Commerce Credit Analyst

JPMorgan Chase, Commercial & Investment Bank · Miami · Jun 2025 to present Adopted by 50+
Problem

An analyst program running on manual diligence. Filings scattered across PDFs, reporting rebuilt by hand every cycle, and senior people spending hours on work a system should be doing.

Built

10+ AI workflow systems that sit inside the daily work, a data ingestion platform now used by 20+ credit professionals, and an internal site of reusable agents that half the team pulls from. Then the training that made all of it stick.

Outcome

Adopted by 50+ analysts and VPs, cutting roughly 70% of the manual work out of parts of our diligence and reporting. Named AI subject matter expert for the analyst program, onboarded 52 incoming analysts, and led 20 live transactions to closing alongside it.

The hard part was trust, not code. Senior people try a tool once, then decide in a day whether it stays.

02

Founder & CEO

WeClean · St. Louis · Dec 2022 to Sep 2023 0 to 100+
Problem

No customers, no operations, no software. A services business in St. Louis that had to be invented and run at the same time.

Built

Operating systems for scheduling, quality control, and customer management, run from start to finish, plus a platform where customers book services and message staff directly.

03

Finance Domain Expert, AI Training Data

Mercor · Remote · Jan to Mar 2026
Problem

Models that read financial filings confidently and get them wrong, with failures that look random until you look closely.

Built

Multi step prompts built from 10-K and 10-Q data, training models on reasoning across documents, numerical analysis, and chart interpretation.

Outcome

Diagnosed the recurring failure patterns and rewrote prompts until output consistency held.

Models fail in patterns, not at random. Finding the pattern is the whole job.
